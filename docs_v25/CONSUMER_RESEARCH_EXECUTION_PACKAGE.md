# RetirePilot 48-Participant Consumer Validation Execution Package

**Actual completed interviews: 0 / 48.** This document specifies collection and analysis, but no participant was contacted or interviewed by this process.

## Recruitment

Use eight *mutually exclusive recruitment buckets* (respondents may have cross-cutting attributes): salaried (8), gig/platform (6), commission/SEP (6), pre-retirement (6), retirees 65+ (6), caregivers/family transitions (5), lower-income/hardship (6), PR/cross-border/complex (5). Total: **48**. Cross-cutting tracking: age 45–49, 50–54, 55–59, 60–64, 65–69, 70+; gender; household size; income stability; housing; CPF confidence; English/Mandarin/Malay/Tamil preference; digital accessibility needs. Do not collect diagnosis-level health data unless essential and consented.

## Standard 45-minute interview procedure

1. 5 min: Plain-language informed consent, voluntary participation, recording choice, withdrawal and retention information.
2. 10 min: Current financial-planning behaviour, major decisions, information sources, trust in financial tools.
3. 10 min: Guided profile intake with synthetic or deliberately rounded example amounts; identify confusing questions and missing options.
4. 10 min: Present an illustrative retirement forecast with explicit assumptions; ask participant to explain what is certain versus estimated.
5. 5 min: Compare job loss, commission decline or care-cost shock; ask participant to describe directional and monetary impacts.
6. 5 min: Task actions, missing-data warnings, comfort with privacy and assisted referrals.

## Required raw evidence per participant

`Study code | recruitment bucket | consent status | language | profile category | tasks completed | critical errors | comprehension items | observed time | verbatim quotes (consented) | priority | dated reviewer`.

No real CPF statements, bank account credentials or Singpass sessions should be used in the current unapproved prototype. Researchers should never request live passwords or OTPs.

## Completion gate

- At least 30–50 real completed sessions, per approved plan; target 48 stratified participants.
- All recruitment quotas reported, including shortfalls. This is qualitative research, not a statistically representative national survey.
- Raw dated study notes, consent provenance, usability severity rating, and independent review of the coverage matrix.
- Unresolved critical misunderstandings or accessibility blocks prevent pilot sign-off.

**Research result currently available:** study protocol only. No findings can be reported as participant evidence.
